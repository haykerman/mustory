# Question catalog

The questions mustory's pages answer, and some it doesn't yet. Each lists the data it needs.

## Built

### YouTube (`youtube-story`)

- **Eras:** long stretches of one artist on repeat. *Watch history.*
- **Song obsessions:** a single video played again and again within a few weeks.
- **Forgotten favorites:** things played a lot, then not at all for 18+ months.
- **Seasons:** each season's top artist and the topic watched far more than usual.
- **Dedications:** videos played across many different years.
- **Soundtrack of each year:** the top three music videos per year.
- **Mood weather:** a mood mix per quarter, from hand-tagged artists.
- **Night owl:** weekday × hour heatmap and after-midnight share per year.
- **Comfort videos:** what gets played mostly after midnight.
- **Taste drift:** how much each pair of years shares in top artists.
- **Flings and lifers:** short intense binges vs artists spread evenly over many years.
- **Gateway videos:** the first video of an artist who later became big.
- **Rabbit holes:** the longest unbroken sessions.
- **Quests:** how-to topics learned over time (guitar, cooking, a language…).
- **Yearly rituals:** artists or themes that come back in the same months every year.
- **On this day:** what was playing on today's date in earlier years.
- **Odd watches:** one-off videos from channels never opened again.
- **Search bar:** most-typed searches.
- **Personality and how it changed:** written from all of the above.

### Spotify (`spotify-story`)

Everything above that fits music, plus:

- **Real listening time,** with overlapping streams from two devices counted once.
- **Loops:** the same song back to back.
- **The skip button:** skip rate over the years, most-skipped songs that kept coming back, never-skipped songs.
- **Autopilot:** how songs started (picked, previous ended, skipped to), and shuffle over time.
- **Music as a tool:** focus and sleep audio, kept out of the taste chapters.
- **Devices:** which machines played the music, year by year.
- **Passport stamps:** countries listened from, and the first song of each trip.
- **Discovery:** new artists per year and how much of each year went to them.
- **Marathons:** the longest sessions, including playlists left running.

### You (`spotify-you`, `youtube-you`)

- **A character:** an invented person who sums up the earned traits, drawn as an animal in headphones picked from the traits, with a novel-style portrait.
- **YouTube version:** watch topics placed in parts of life (learning, making, stories, news, comedy, gaming…) and how-to hobbies feed the life guesses.
- **Traits:** 25 fixed traits scored 0–100 from taste and habits, so friends can compare.
- **Taste directions:** genres mapped to five directions (Mellow, Unpretentious, Sophisticated, Intense, Contemporary), loosely after music-psychology research.
- **Life guesses:** friends, parties, work, holidays, karaoke, "you'd probably never…", each with its music clue.
- **This or that, a day in the life, green and red flags, a share card.**

### Both (`youtube-vs-spotify`)

- Which app does the work each year.
- Hour-of-day and weekend split per app.
- Which app finds artists first, and how long they take to cross over.
- Which app "owns" each shared artist.
- Artists that live on one app only.
- Songs played on both.

## Not built yet

- **Find that video where someone explained X:** needs video transcripts or descriptions fetched, then a full-text or semantic index.
- **What did I learn about a topic, and what's missing:** needs transcripts.
- **Which videos contradicted each other:** needs transcripts.
- **Did a video lead to a project or a bookmark:** needs another data source (git history, saved links).
- **Search vs watch:** what was searched but never watched in the following minutes (YouTube search history is already in the export).
- **Mood from audio:** Spotify's audio features (energy, valence) per track, instead of hand-tagged moods; needs the Spotify Web API.
- **Apple Music, Last.fm, SoundCloud** exports, using the same page shape.

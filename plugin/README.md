# mustory

Your music and video history, told back to you. mustory turns your own YouTube and Spotify data downloads into a scrolling story page you can make any time of year: your eras and obsessions, forgotten favorites, the songs you skip and the ones you loop, when you listen, where you traveled, and how your taste changed over the years.

## Skills

- **start**: the front door. Works out what you want, finds your downloads, and gives step-by-step instructions for getting your data from Google Takeout and Spotify.
- **youtube-story**: a story of your YouTube watching, from a Google Takeout export.
- **spotify-story**: a story of your Spotify listening, from Spotify's data download (Extended streaming history is best).
- **spotify-you**: a playful page about you rather than the music: an invented character, guesses about your life from your taste and habits ("your friends probably think you're…"), trait badges and a share card.
- **youtube-you**: the same playful page from your YouTube history: your music taste plus what you watch, the hobbies you taught yourself and your late nights.
- **youtube-vs-spotify**: how the two apps fit into your life: which one you use when, where you find artists first, and the songs you play everywhere.

## How to use

Say "help me get started with mustory", or ask for your YouTube or Spotify story. Claude does the rest and checks every number in the story against your data.

## Privacy

Everything runs on your computer. Your history is never uploaded or sent anywhere. The scripts don't keep IP addresses, never list private-session plays, and delete their temporary copies when they finish.

## Requirements

Python 3.9 or newer. The `start` skill helps you install it if it's missing.
